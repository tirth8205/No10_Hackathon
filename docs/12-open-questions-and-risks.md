# 12. Open questions and risks

## 12.1 Questions for MoJ and the LAA

| # | Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Why was the paragraph 14 "we provided" example rejected? Is it because "we" is not the IDVA personally confirming support, or for another reason? | Decides how the engine treats IDVA letters |
| 2 | Is the rest of the promised data pack available? It was meant to include the application form, police and court documents, expired or poor-quality examples, provider submissions and caseworker feedback notes | Better testing, especially for paragraphs 1 to 10 |
| 3 | Can LAA staff check a sample of our synthetic labels? | Confirms our reading of the guidance |
| 4 | Would the LAA accept a provider's check result attached to a submission, or prefer to run only its own? | Shapes link 2 |
| 5 | How would link 2 connect to the LAA's current application systems? | Integration and reference mapping |
| 6 | How are Controlled Work evidence audits carried out today? | Format of the audit-ready file note |
| 7 | Who would run the applicant app: MoJ, the LAA, providers, or a third party? | Controller responsibilities, trust and branding |
| 8 | Has the DAPN pilot area list changed since the guidance was published? | Paragraph 6A checks |
| 9 | Are there more examples of what caseworkers accept for "specific matters relied on" in paragraphs 16 and 17? | This is the most judgement-heavy requirement |
| 10 | What retention periods apply to link 1 and link 2 data? | Data model and data protection |

## 12.2 Risks

| Risk | Impact | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| False approval of ineligible evidence | High | Medium | Quotes required for every "met"; refer when unsure; independent LAA check; people decide; measure false approvals separately |
| False rejection of valid evidence | High | Medium | Substance over form; tolerate harmless defects; refer rather than fail when unclear; test with benign-defect cases |
| Applicant discovered by the abuser through the app | Very high | Medium | Safety controls in [09](09-safety-privacy-and-security.md); review with domestic abuse specialists |
| Labels reflect our reading, not the LAA's | Medium | Medium | LAA spot-check; final test on MoJ's own examples |
| Synthetic data is cleaner than real documents | Medium | High | Test with real anonymised documents before any live use; measure text recognition separately |
| Rules change (new guidance version or pilot) | Medium | Medium | Rules held as versioned data; version recorded on every result |
| Over-reliance by users | Medium | Medium | Show the quotes; no automatic decisions; monitor agreement in the decision log |
| Sensitive data exposure | Very high | Low | Separate links, minimum data, encryption, audit, DPIA before live use |
| Hackathon scope too large | Medium | High | Build the checking engine, solicitor workspace and caseworker view first. Prototype the applicant app as a clickable mock-up if time is short |
