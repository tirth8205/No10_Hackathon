# 13. Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| A | The applicant for legal aid, in the wording of Schedule 1 |
| B | The person with whom A was in a family relationship giving rise to the need for legal aid, usually the other party |
| Bind over | A court order requiring a person to keep the peace, sometimes used instead of prosecution |
| Cafcass | Children and Family Court Advisory and Support Service (Cafcass Cymru in Wales) |
| Case reference | Our neutral reference for a case on link 1, shared by the applicant and solicitor |
| Controlled Work | Legal aid work where the provider decides eligibility and keeps the evidence on file |
| CPS | Crown Prosecution Service |
| CW1 | The Controlled Work application form |
| DAPN | Domestic Abuse Protection Notice, given by police under section 22 of the Domestic Abuse Act 2021 (pilot areas only) |
| DASH | A domestic abuse risk assessment checklist widely used by police and support services |
| DLAC | Director of Legal Aid Casework, the statutory decision maker at the LAA |
| DVPN | Domestic Violence Protection Notice, issued by police under section 24 of the Crime and Security Act 2010 |
| Element | One requirement of a Schedule 1 paragraph that a document must show |
| Evidence author | The professional or body that writes a piece of evidence |
| Finding of fact | A court's formal decision on disputed facts, such as whether abuse took place |
| IDVA | Independent domestic violence advisor or advocate |
| ISVA | Independent sexual violence advisor or advocate |
| LAA | Legal Aid Agency |
| LAA reference | The LAA's own reference for a Licensed Work application, used on link 2 |
| LASPO | Legal Aid, Sentencing and Punishment of Offenders Act 2012 |
| Licensed Work | Legal aid work where the provider applies to the LAA, which decides |
| Link 1 | The shared connection between the applicant and solicitor |
| Link 2 | The separate connection between the solicitor and the LAA |
| MARAC | Multi-agency risk assessment conference |
| N117 | The court's General Form of Undertaking |
| Nil assessed | A Controlled Work claim that is not paid because the requirements were not met |
| Non-molestation order | A protective injunction under section 42 of the Family Law Act 1996 |
| Pass, fail, refer | Document and case outcomes. Refer means a person must decide or more information is needed |
| Provider | A firm of solicitors holding a legal aid contract |
| Schedule 1 | The schedule to the Civil Legal Aid (Procedure) Regulations 2012 listing domestic abuse evidence |
| Schedule 2 | The schedule listing evidence relating to the protection of children |
| Undertaking | A promise to the court, which can be given instead of a protective injunction |
| Witness Care Unit | The joint police and CPS unit that keeps victims and witnesses informed about a case |
